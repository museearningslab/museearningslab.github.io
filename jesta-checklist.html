You Do NOT Need JESTA in 2027 — Japan Entry Checklist (Scam Warning)

November 24, 2026

If a website is asking you to pay for a "JESTA application" for a 2026 or 2027 trip to Japan, close the tab. JESTA is planned for fiscal 2028. Here is the evidence, how the scam works, and the short list of things you actually need to enter Japan.

Search results and social media posts are increasingly full of sites offering to "process your JESTA application" for travel to Japan — for a fee, and in exchange for your passport details. This article exists to save you money and your identity documents: for any trip in 2026 or 2027, JESTA does not exist yet and there is nothing to apply for.

The facts, in one table

QuestionAnswer
What is JESTA?Japan's planned electronic travel authorization system (similar in concept to the US ESTA), announced by the Japanese government.
When does it start?Planned for introduction in fiscal 2028.
Do I need it for a 2026 or 2027 trip?No. There is no application, no fee, and no official application website for travelers yet.
A site says my trip will be refused without it. True?No. That is the scam's pressure tactic. Entry requirements for 2027 do not include JESTA.
When should I start checking?If you are traveling in fiscal 2028 or later, check official Japanese government sources (Immigration Services Agency / Ministry of Foreign Affairs) closer to your trip.

How the scam works

  1. The site ranks for searches like "JESTA application" or "Japan travel authorization" and copies official-looking design — flags, crests, government-style wording.
  2. It creates urgency: "apply now," "processing takes weeks," "travel may be denied."
  3. It collects exactly what an identity thief wants: full passport details, date of birth, and a card payment for a "processing fee."
  4. You receive a meaningless confirmation PDF — or nothing. The real damage is the data you handed over.
Red flags: any site charging money for JESTA today; any site that does not end in an official Japanese government domain (go.jp); any "agent" promising to expedite an authorization that does not exist. If you already paid one, contact your card issuer about a chargeback and monitor for misuse of your passport details.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need JESTA to enter Japan in 2027?

No. JESTA, Japan's planned electronic travel authorization, is scheduled for introduction in fiscal 2028. For any trip in 2026 or 2027 there is nothing to apply for.

A website is asking me to pay for a JESTA application. Is it legitimate?

No. Because JESTA does not exist yet, any site charging a fee to apply for JESTA is at best useless and at worst a scam collecting passport details and card numbers. Do not enter personal information and do not pay.

What do I actually need to enter Japan in 2027?

A valid passport, and a visa or a visa exemption covering your nationality and purpose of travel. You can complete immigration and customs declarations in advance with Visit Japan Web. Requirements vary by nationality, so confirm them on official pages before you travel.

When will JESTA start, and will it cost money?

JESTA is planned for fiscal 2028. Details such as the application process and any fee have not been finalized for travelers. Check official Japanese government announcements closer to your trip rather than commercial application sites.
